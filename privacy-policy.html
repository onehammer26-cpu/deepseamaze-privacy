Privacy Policy - Deep Sea Maze

Last updated: November 2026

Deep Sea Maze ("the game", "we") is a single-player puzzle game made by One Hamier Studios. This policy explains what information the game uses and how.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

So you can continue where you left off, the game saves the following only on your device: your current level and maze, stars earned, pearls, hints, unlocked colours and trails, daily-reward streak, your settings (music, sound, vibration) and which tips you have seen. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

Permissions

The game does not ask for any special permissions. It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location or the internet for its own use.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience (13 and over). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Sharing

We do not collect, sell or share personal information. If you tap Share, the game hands a short message with the Play Store link to the app you choose.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, where required, ask for your consent again.
